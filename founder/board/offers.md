# Offers lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- One buyer, one job: "Every client site. Every page, on record." Paid plans lead with monitoring and history, which free tools lack.
- The Agency stack is the best part. Branded share and before/after links make the agency look good to its client, at little cost to deliver.
- Effort is low: Google sign-in, login pages captured without a password, and cookie banners hidden and animations settled before comparing. If that works, it answers Visualping's main complaint, false alerts.
- The free tier offers what the panel asked for most: a run on your own site, phone included.
- The scarcity is real: 100 Lifetime seats with a live count.
- The downside is small: break-even at about 6 subscribers (CFO estimate).

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Likelihood is still zero.** Nobody outside has run it, it is not on a public domain, there are no reviews, and trust was the top reason simulated buyers passed (9 of 20). Seven of ten conditions are done, all desk work; the two undone (outside users, paying users) are the only ones that create proof.
2. **The pain comes and goes.** 9 of 20 already use their own extension or script, or need it only a few times a year. Monitoring pays off weeks after signup, so the wait is longest where the money is. Churn is unknown: real users answer it, then the CFO.
3. **Cheap by accident.** Studio is $9 for 125 pages; Stillio starts at $29 for 5. With no proof, a low price looks like a hobby. About $7.40 a month per subscriber leaves little room for paid acquisition or a strong guarantee. Customer acquisition cost is unknown until a channel is tested.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which 5 agencies run it on a client site in the next two weeks, and what clean-capture rate would make you stop?
- Which moment makes an agency pay: a dispute, a redesign launch, a handover or the monthly report? Which does the page lead with?
- What proof is on the page the day it goes public?
- Can the guarantee name the outcome ("if it cannot capture your client's site cleanly, you pay nothing"), and can the CFO afford it?
- A maxed Studio or Lifetime account can hold up to 5,000 full-page PNGs (125 pages × 4 devices × 10 runs), 5-25 GB. What does that cost? (CFO.)
- Would Agency at double the price, with the first client sites set up for them, be the obvious choice? (Real users, then pricing.)

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Live on a public domain, with 5-10 target agencies on their own client sites. Record clean-capture and false-alert rates against a bar set in advance.
2. Rewrite the promise and the guarantee in those users' words, with a clean-capture guarantee the CFO signs off.
3. One channel tested to about 10 paying users, with the cost of each recorded, before any new feature.
4. Before the first Lifetime sale, cap storage per Lifetime account or have the CFO price a maxed one.
