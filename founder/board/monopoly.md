# Monopoly lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- **The desk work is done.** Every first-meeting condition that needed no stranger has been met: one buyer (small agencies), named competitors, monitoring over one-off capture, Lifetime capped at 100, costs estimated. Break-even at about 6 paying subscribers (CFO estimate) means a very small market can keep it alive.
- **A small market is named.** A dated, client-facing record of the sites an agency looks after, with branded before/after links. Narrow enough to own, which this lens wants first.
- **Two candidates for "ten times better", both unproven.** Volume per dollar: Studio covers 5 sites × 25 pages for $9; Stillio charges $29 for 5 pages. Alert accuracy: Visualping's main complaint is false alerts, and ShotDomain hides cookie banners and settles animations before comparing.
- **History is built, not planned:** schedules, kept runs, compare any two dates.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Still no distribution.** No public domain, no outside user, no channel, no customer; 0 of 20 simulated buyers bought, 9 on trust. Unchanged since the first meeting, and this lens counts distribution as half the business. How the first 100 agencies arrive and what each costs is unknown (CFO step, once a channel is tested).
2. **The edge is price, not proof.** A pages-per-dollar gap is a price fight an incumbent can end by editing a pricing page. Alert accuracy could become real know-how, but no clean-capture or false-alert rate has been measured on one outside site. Until then it is a claim.
3. **The plans cap the moat.** "Every page, on record" sits on keeping the last 10 runs per site (Studio) or 30 (Agency): on a daily schedule, 10 or 30 days. Leaving costs a customer little. And if Agency's branded links carry no ShotDomain mark, the one built-in loop for new agencies (clients seeing the tool) is off.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which ten agencies run it on their own client sites first, and how do you reach them?
- On the same pages, what is your false-alert rate against Visualping's? (outside-user test, competitor research)
- What retention and run frequency do Stillio and CaptureDeck include at those prices? Is the gap real like for like? (competitor research)
- Is the 10/30-run cap a storage decision? What would a year per site cost? (CFO)
- Does any share link carry a ShotDomain mark that brings new agencies in?
- Why now: what makes this possible or needed today?
- What stops Visualping adding banner hiding in month six?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
Not applicable; the vote is PASS. These would turn it into FUND IF:
- Live on a public domain with 5-10 outside agencies on their own client sites, clean-capture and false-alert rates recorded against Visualping on the same pages.
- One channel tested, at a cost per paying agency the CFO step shows is earned back at $9-$29 a month.
- History deep enough to grow with tenure (for example a year per site, if the CFO shows it is affordable), so staying gets more valuable every month.
