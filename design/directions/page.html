ShotDomain: four looks for Template piles

Template piles, four looks

One mechanic, four ways to see it. Every direction shows the same site (example.com, 412 pages in six templates, six pages that break their template), the same moment in each screen, and every real ShotDomain function. The big screen is live: try it.

A. Still life

Each template is a real object: a ream of miniature pages on a plinth, one ream per device, standing on its own colour field in window light. Printed edges show how much is captured. Pages that break the template float off their pile with a pencilled note.

Try this

  • Hover a pile: its top pages riffle.
  • Click Blog post: the pages are dealt into a spread and the odd ones slide into their tray.
  • Click any page for a full-size preview on Desktop and iPhone.
  • Pick One-off pages: its pencil outline turns into paper.
  • Turn off iPhone 14 Pro: every phone ream leaves its plinth.
  • Type benchmarks in Find one exact page.

Where everything lives

  • Enter a domain and start: the start screen; Change site in the top bar.
  • Piles from URL patterns: one colour field per template, with its pattern and count.
  • Pick or unpick a pile: the Picked toggle on each field.
  • One exact page: Find one exact page, bottom left; it opens the pile and the preview.
  • Devices: top bar; each picked device adds a ream to every plinth.
  • Options: top bar (full page, hide cookie banners, log in first, custom CSS).
  • Live progress: printed edges on each ream and the tick strip under each name; totals bottom centre.
  • Failed pages and retry: red sheets sticking out of the Docs ream, Retry under its name.
  • Odd pages: float beside their pile after capture; in the open pile they sit in their own tray with the reason.
  • Review across devices: open a pile; Desktop and iPhone prints side by side, Review on toggles.
  • Full-size preview: click any print.
  • Download ZIP, share link (30 days), stop sharing: bottom right.

Trade-off: the most beautiful at rest and the calmest to use, but the least new in how it moves. Six 3D piles cost more to draw than flat cards, so the art is drawn once and only the top sheets ever move.

Riffle

Every pile is a real stack of your pages, lit by one window. Run the pointer across a stack and it flicks through like a flip book. When a pile finishes, the pages that break its template jump out of the stack into the Odd ones lane.

Try this

  • Move across the Blog post stack, slowly then fast. Let go and the pages slap back down.
  • Click a stack: it spreads into every page on Desktop and iPhone in one move.
  • Click a page there for the full-size preview.
  • Type /blog/benchmarks in Find a page.
  • Watch Docs: pages drop onto it as they are captured. Retry its 2 failed pages.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a stack, arrow keys riffle, Enter opens.

Palette

Cobalt set #2547d0, paper #ffffff, sky #c9d6ff, flag yellow #ffd23f for odd ones, coral #ff7a66 for failures, ink #0d1a5c.

Type

Funnel Display 800 for names and counts, Funnel Sans for everything you read. Both embedded.

Trade-off: the riffle is a joy with a pointer, but a stack only shows about ten pages at a time; past that you open the pile. Real 3D in CSS needs care to stay smooth with hundreds of pages, so each stack draws ten sheets and a ream, never every page.

C. The sorting office

ShotDomain as a mail sorting room. Each template is a pigeonhole in a painted steel frame, its count on a brass label. Each captured page is a postcard that slides into its hole. Every action is something a sorter would do with their hands.

Every action is a ritual

StartWrite the domain on a delivery note and drop it in the in-tray. The crawl sorts addresses into holes by URL pattern.
PickRoll a hole's shutter up to pick it, down to leave it out. Look up one exact page like an address.
CapturePostcards slide into their holes. A failed page comes back stamped Returned, with a Retry tag.
Odd pagesCards that break the template land in the Doesn't fit tray with a red stamp naming why.
ReviewPull a bundle out and untie the string: its pages lay out on the table, Desktop beside iPhone.
ZIP, linkThe ZIP is a parcel. The link is an envelope posted to your client, 30 days on the postmark. Recall stops it.

Try this

  • Click the Blog post hole, then Put back.
  • Pull the strap on One-off pages to roll its shutter up.
  • Retry the 2 returned Docs pages.
  • Stamp iPad Pro 11 on the docket.
  • Type /docs/api in Look up a page.
  • Recall the envelope, then post it again.

Where everything lives

  • Domain and start: the delivery note in the in-tray (New delivery).
  • Piles, patterns, counts and live progress: the holes and their brass labels.
  • Pick or unpick: each hole's shutter strap. One page: Look up a page, top right.
  • Devices and options (full page, cookie banners, login session, custom CSS): the docket on the wall.
  • Failed pages and retry: the Returned cards in Docs.
  • Odd pages: the Doesn't fit tray. Review across devices: open a hole. Full size: click any card.
  • Download ZIP: the parcel. Share link, 30 days, and stopping it: the envelope.

Palette and type

Archivo with its width axis for labels and reading, Big Shoulders Stencil for painted signs, Kalam for handwriting on notes and chalk.

Trade-off: the world is unforgettable and every function has an object, but it costs space. Six holes fill the wall; a site with 20 templates needs a second bay or a scrolling frame, and a client has to enjoy the metaphor.

D The likeness field

Grouping pages into piles is a measure of how alike they are, so D shows that measure. Each of the 412 pages is a point, placed by how alike its layout is to the rest. Templates gather into constellations. A page that breaks its template drifts out on a thread as long as the difference, with the reason at its end.

Try this

  • Click a constellation, or scroll up over it, to open it. Scroll down to go back.
  • In an opened pile, hover a page for the loupe; click it for full size.
  • Retry the 2 failed Docs pages and watch them ignite.
  • Search for /blog/benchmarks.
  • Turn off iPhone 14 Pro, then open a pile.

Where everything lives

  • Domain and start: the start screen; the domain stays in the top bar with Change.
  • Piles from URL patterns: each constellation's label (name, pattern, count).
  • Pick or unpick: the bead on each label. One-off pages starts unpicked and dim.
  • Find one exact page: search, top right.
  • Devices and options: beads on the two threads at the bottom.
  • Live progress: each constellation's ring fills and its points ignite; totals bottom right.
  • Failed pages and retry: the flickering Docs points and Retry on the Docs label.
  • Odd pages: drift out on threads after capture, reason at the end.
  • Review across devices: open a pile; every tile is Desktop and iPhone.
  • Full-size preview: click any tile or odd page.
  • Download ZIP and the 30 day share link (and Stop sharing): top right.

Palette and type

Gloock for names, Instrument Sans for everything you read and press, DM Mono only for URLs.

Trade-off: the most unexpected of the four and true to how piles are found, but a field asks people to learn a new map. The labels, the bottom threads and the opened spiral keep every action one click away.
