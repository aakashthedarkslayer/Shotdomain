Riffle

Every pile is a real stack of your pages, lit by one window. Run the pointer across a stack and it flicks through like a flip book. When a pile finishes, the pages that break its template jump out of the stack into the Odd ones lane.

Try this

  • Move across the Blog post stack, slowly then fast. Let go and the pages slap back down.
  • Click a stack: it spreads into every page on Desktop and iPhone in one move.
  • Click a page there for the full-size preview.
  • Type /blog/benchmarks in Find a page.
  • Watch Docs: pages drop onto it as they are captured. Retry its 2 failed pages.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a stack, arrow keys riffle, Enter opens.

Palette

Cobalt set #2547d0, paper #ffffff, sky #c9d6ff, flag yellow #ffd23f for odd ones, coral #ff7a66 for failures, ink #0d1a5c.

Type

Funnel Display 800 for names and counts, Funnel Sans for everything you read. Both embedded.

Trade-off: the riffle is a joy with a pointer, but a stack only shows about ten pages at a time; past that you open the pile. Real 3D in CSS needs care to stay smooth with hundreds of pages, so each stack draws ten sheets and a ream, never every page.
