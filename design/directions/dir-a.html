A. Still life

Each template is a real object: a ream of miniature pages on a plinth, one ream per device, standing on its own colour field in window light. Printed edges show how much is captured. Pages that break the template float off their pile with a pencilled note.

Try this

  • Hover a pile: its top pages riffle.
  • Click Blog post: the pages are dealt into a spread and the odd ones slide into their tray.
  • Click any page for a full-size preview on Desktop and iPhone.
  • Pick One-off pages: its pencil outline turns into paper.
  • Turn off iPhone 14 Pro: every phone ream leaves its plinth.
  • Type benchmarks in Find one exact page.

Where everything lives

  • Enter a domain and start: the start screen; Change site in the top bar.
  • Piles from URL patterns: one colour field per template, with its pattern and count.
  • Pick or unpick a pile: the Picked toggle on each field.
  • One exact page: Find one exact page, bottom left; it opens the pile and the preview.
  • Devices: top bar; each picked device adds a ream to every plinth.
  • Options: top bar (full page, hide cookie banners, log in first, custom CSS).
  • Live progress: printed edges on each ream and the tick strip under each name; totals bottom centre.
  • Failed pages and retry: red sheets sticking out of the Docs ream, Retry under its name.
  • Odd pages: float beside their pile after capture; in the open pile they sit in their own tray with the reason.
  • Review across devices: open a pile; Desktop and iPhone prints side by side, Review on toggles.
  • Full-size preview: click any print.
  • Download ZIP, share link (30 days), stop sharing: bottom right.

Trade-off: the most beautiful at rest and the calmest to use, but the least new in how it moves. Six 3D piles cost more to draw than flat cards, so the art is drawn once and only the top sheets ever move.
