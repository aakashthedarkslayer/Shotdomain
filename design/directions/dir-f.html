F The overprint

The site's own shape is the layout: one sheet per template, its area the page count. Each sheet is an overprint of every page in its pile. Pages that fit print in one ink, in register. A page that breaks its template rises out of the pile with its pink plate slipped by how unlike the pile it is, so you see which pages don't line up before you read a word.

Real, not drawn

Every print is a real capture of a 412-page demo site, made with ShotDomain's Desktop and iPhone 14 Pro presets. The odd pages were found by a real check (layout likeness against the pile, plus broken images, content off the page, and a header, sidebar or font unlike the pile): it found the six planted pages and no others.

Where everything lives

  • Enter a domain and start: the start screen, Capture site.
  • Piles from URL patterns with counts: the sheets, labelled under the bed line.
  • Pick or unpick a pile: One-off pages shows Pick; every opened pile has Leave out.
  • Find one exact page: Find a page, in the bar.
  • Devices and options: the sentence in the sky; each underlined word opens or toggles it (login session and custom CSS included).
  • Live progress per pile: the drum line moving down the sheet, and the count.
  • Failed pages and retry: under Docs.
  • Odd pages after capture: they rise out of their pile; hover one to see it in register.
  • Review a pile across devices: open it; the device chips switch the frames.
  • Full-size preview: Full size on every page.
  • Download ZIP and the 30-day share link (and Stop sharing): the bar, ready when printing ends.

Inks

Trade-off: the overprint is honest only if the check behind it is right. A pile of pages that all differ a lot (one-off pages) has no template, so it gets no overprint and no odd pages.
