D The likeness field

Grouping pages into piles is a measure of how alike they are, so D shows that measure. Each of the 412 pages is a point, placed by how alike its layout is to the rest. Templates gather into constellations. A page that breaks its template drifts out on a thread as long as the difference, with the reason at its end.

Try this

  • Click a constellation, or scroll up over it, to open it. Scroll down to go back.
  • In an opened pile, hover a page for the loupe; click it for full size.
  • Retry the 2 failed Docs pages and watch them ignite.
  • Search for /blog/benchmarks.
  • Turn off iPhone 14 Pro, then open a pile.

Where everything lives

  • Domain and start: the start screen; the domain stays in the top bar with Change.
  • Piles from URL patterns: each constellation's label (name, pattern, count).
  • Pick or unpick: the bead on each label. One-off pages starts unpicked and dim.
  • Find one exact page: search, top right.
  • Devices and options: beads on the two threads at the bottom.
  • Live progress: each constellation's ring fills and its points ignite; totals bottom right.
  • Failed pages and retry: the flickering Docs points and Retry on the Docs label.
  • Odd pages: drift out on threads after capture, reason at the end.
  • Review across devices: open a pile; every tile is Desktop and iPhone.
  • Full-size preview: click any tile or odd page.
  • Download ZIP and the 30 day share link (and Stop sharing): top right.

Palette and type

Gloock for names, Instrument Sans for everything you read and press, DM Mono only for URLs.

Trade-off: the most unexpected of the four and true to how piles are found, but a field asks people to learn a new map. The labels, the bottom threads and the opened spiral keep every action one click away.
