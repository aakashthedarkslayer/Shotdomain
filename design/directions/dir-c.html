C. The sorting office

ShotDomain as a mail sorting room. Each template is a pigeonhole in a painted steel frame, its count on a brass label. Each captured page is a postcard that slides into its hole. Every action is something a sorter would do with their hands.

Every action is a ritual

StartWrite the domain on a delivery note and drop it in the in-tray. The crawl sorts addresses into holes by URL pattern.
PickRoll a hole's shutter up to pick it, down to leave it out. Look up one exact page like an address.
CapturePostcards slide into their holes. A failed page comes back stamped Returned, with a Retry tag.
Odd pagesCards that break the template land in the Doesn't fit tray with a red stamp naming why.
ReviewPull a bundle out and untie the string: its pages lay out on the table, Desktop beside iPhone.
ZIP, linkThe ZIP is a parcel. The link is an envelope posted to your client, 30 days on the postmark. Recall stops it.

Where everything lives

  • Domain and start: the delivery note in the in-tray (New delivery).
  • Piles, patterns, counts and live progress: the holes and their brass labels.
  • Pick or unpick: each hole's shutter strap. One page: Look up a page, top right.
  • Devices and options (full page, cookie banners, login session, custom CSS): the docket on the wall.
  • Failed pages and retry: the Returned cards in Docs.
  • Odd pages: the Doesn't fit tray. Review across devices: open a hole. Full size: click any card.
  • Download ZIP: the parcel. Share link, 30 days, and stopping it: the envelope.

Palette and type

Archivo with its width axis for labels and reading, Big Shoulders Stencil for painted signs, Kalam for handwriting on notes and chalk.

Trade-off: the world is unforgettable and every function has an object, but it costs space. Six holes fill the wall; a site with 20 templates needs a second bay or a scrolling frame, and a client has to enjoy the metaphor.
