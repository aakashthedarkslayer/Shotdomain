E. The sorting office, printed

C's sorting frame holding B's paper reams, printed on a risograph in ShotDomain's own inks. Each template is a pigeonhole; each hole holds a ream of its captured pages, as thick as its count, and you riffle it to see them. Pink is the second plate: it marks what you pick, where you are, and every page that is odd or failed.

Every action is a ritual, with its plain name

StartWrite the domain on the delivery note. Addresses sort into holes by URL pattern.New delivery: change site
PickTick a hole's label and its shutter rolls up; untick and it rolls down. Look up one page like an address.
CaptureSheets land on the ream; Docs is still filling. A failed page comes back stamped Returned.Retry: capture again
BrowseMove across a ream (or use the arrow keys) and it riffles; the rail below names each page, so nothing covers the flip.
Odd pagesWhen a pile finishes, pages that break its template are stamped in pink with how alike they are, and drop onto the shelf.
ReviewPull the bundle out and untie it: Desktop beside iPhone, numbered in capture order.Put back: back to piles
ZIP, linkThe parcel is the ZIP. The envelope is posted to your client, 30 days on the postmark.Recall: stop sharing

Where everything lives

  • Domain and start, find one page, devices, options and overall progress: the docket strip on the right.
  • Piles by URL pattern, counts, pick, live progress, failed pages and retry: the holes and their labels.
  • Odd pages, the ZIP and the share link (30 days, Recall to stop): the shelf at the bottom of the frame.
  • Review across devices: click a ream. Full size: click any page.

Palette and type

Anybody only: 62% width at 900 for heads, 75% with tabular figures for counts and paths, normal width to read. Screenshots stay in true colour, keylined in blue.

Trade-off: the world is calmer than C and keeps every ritual, but the frame is still fixed at six holes; a site with 15 templates needs a second bay that slides in, or smaller holes. The riffle needs a pointer or the arrow keys to be discovered, so the autoplay shows it once.
