# Stills round 2: ui-critic report on the hybrid (E)

E First glance: 6/10 (below bar). E Wow: 6/10 (below bar). Verdict: RETHINK.

Same app as previous: YES against round 1 C (same 3x2 holes, labels, trays, parcel and envelope; the material changed, not the design). NO against B and NO against today's app.

- Motion 6: the riffle (pink thimble, "Page 5 of 214") and the stamped sheets jumping out are real rituals, but the sheets flip as rigid planes.
- Hierarchy 6: calmer than C, but pink is spread over about 20 marks, so the odd pages (the product's insight) don't win; "312" is printed three times.
- Craft 6: CSS 3D reams on one vanishing point, ink-masked stamps and the envelope are custom; but every page is a grey-bar wireframe, the holes are flat lavender boxes, the frame's grain reads as TV static, and the "Returned" stamp can't be read.
- Real app 8: every function has a plain label; no Pause or Stop capture.
- Scale 5: reams get thicker with the page count, but six fixed holes, and the opened bundle shows 6 of 211.
- Skeleton: standard (a 3x2 tile grid, a right settings rail and an action footer). Seen before: YES. Better than round 1 C: NO.

Top fixes:
1. Real prints: real captures through scripts/riso-print.mjs on every top sheet and thumbnail; true colour only in the opened bundle.
2. Size the holes by page count (square root of each count: Blog 44%, Docs 35%, Product 21%; Home, Pricing and One-off as a row of narrow slots): the site's own shape becomes the layout.
3. Paper, not lavender: paper hole interiors with a riso-blue halftone for walls and floor; the frame solid riso blue with low-frequency ink mottling.
4. Give pink to the odd pages: shrink pick marks; fan the odd pages large on the shelf with solid pink stamp plates; a readable "Returned".
5. Type and shelf: three sizes per region, nothing under 12 px, one "312", fill the tray.

Bolder version: the misregistration is the diff. Pages that fit their template print in register; a page that breaks it comes off the drum with its pink plate shifted by how unlike it is (58% alike drifts 10 px), so a still shows which pages don't line up before you read a word.

Gap to 9/9: 3 points on each axis. The pigeonhole tile grid caps Wow at 7. Getting to 9 needs real riso-printed captures, a layout drawn from the site's own shape, and one signature image that only this product could make. Keep the mechanic, the plain-word labels, the docket, the stamps and the two inks; replace the even 3x2 composition.
