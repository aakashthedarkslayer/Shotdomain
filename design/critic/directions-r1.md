# Directions round 1: ui-critic report (one pass across A to D)

Best of the set: First glance 7/10, Wow 7/10 (B, C and D tie; A is 6/5). Verdict: RETHINK, all four below bar.

The set fails the range rule because A and B are the same type: white CSS-3D reams on a colour field, a tile grid and a right rail. None of the four keeps the Riso proof identity in DESIGN.md (Anybody, two inks, no black, no glows), so the winner needs a DESIGN.md rewrite or a translation into those inks.

Capture notes: A's riffle.png is identical to rest.png (no riffle state captured); B's art.png is mid-flight, not a rest still (312 vs 313); for C, art.png was scored because rest.png is the whole preview page.

## A Still life (art-led)
A First glance: 6/10. A Wow: 5/10.
- Motion: deal and tray read as fades and flat translations, no weight.
- Hierarchy: Blog and Docs land first, but six equal pastel fields compete.
- Craft: real 3D reams, but every sheet is a grey-bar wireframe; the handwritten notes look like Excalidraw.
- Real app: every function findable in plain words. Best of the four.
- Scale: the hand-composed bento breaks at about 12 templates; an opened pile shows 6 of 211, then a plain grid.
- Skeleton: standard. Seen before: YES (pastel bento with isometric art). Same app as previous: NO.
- Fixes: real captures on each ream's top sheet; one continuous table instead of six tiles, reams sized by count, one light; one ground and one accent (riso blue), no pastels or near-black buttons.
- Bolder: WebGL paper reams under one window light with real screenshots as textures; the light warms as capture completes.

## B Riffle (art and signature motion)
B First glance: 7/10. B Wow: 7/10.
- Motion: the riffle is worth recording and the odd page arcs into the rail, but the tooltip covers the flip.
- Hierarchy: cobalt and white paper read at once, but the Blog stack bleeds off the left edge.
- Craft: convincing sheet edges; queued piles drawn as dashed wireframes look unfinished; gradient ground.
- Real app: device toggles are icons only (iPhone and Pixel look identical); Stop sharing hidden behind Share.
- Scale: each stack draws ten sheets, so the count is a label, not a picture.
- Skeleton: standard. Seen before: YES (card grid with a side rail; hover-scrub like macOS Stacks). Same app: NO.
- Fixes: pull the Blog stack inside the gutter, draw queued piles as faint paper reams; one accent (print pink for flags and failures), flat ground; label the device toggles, real captures on the top sheets.
- Bolder: a page-curl shader driven by pointer speed with real screenshots; odd pages won't lie flat, so the riffle finds them.

## C The sorting office (a world)
C First glance: 7/10. C Wow: 7/10.
- Motion: untie, stamp and drop are real rituals, but the stamps are crude blocks and the untie plays small.
- Hierarchy: everything is loud (brass, red stamps, clipboard, parcel, postcard); the eye has nowhere to land.
- Craft: the most custom of the four (SVG rivets, shutter, hinges); the desk reads as grey water; inconsistent light.
- Real app: every function is there, but metaphor names (Recall, New delivery) blur Stop sharing and Change site.
- Scale: the 3x2 frame is fixed, so 15 templates shrink the holes.
- Skeleton: new. Seen before: NO (new in this category). Same app: NO.
- Fixes: four materials (steel green, brass, kraft, paper), red only for odd and failed; give the frame 70% of the canvas, a 280px docket strip, one shelf of trays; one condensed face and one reading face, function words under each metaphor.
- Bolder: URLs drop as postcards down a chute with real physics, the stamp bleeds ink, the share envelope is dragged into a postbox.

## D The likeness field (wild card)
D First glance: 7/10. D Wow: 7/10.
- Motion: dots exploding into 214 tiles is the moment worth recording.
- Hierarchy: headline, then the Blog ring; the landing-page paragraph steals prime space.
- Craft: a custom field with progress rings and a loupe; Share and ZIP labels touch their borders.
- Real app: every function is there, but pick is a sparkle glyph that doesn't read as a toggle.
- Scale: points scale best, but labels collide past about 8 templates.
- Skeleton: new. Seen before: YES (embedding projector, Nomic Atlas, Obsidian graph). Same app: NO.
- Fixes: points become tiny real thumbnails; remove the headline and paragraph; padded buttons, a visible pick checkbox, no glow halos.
- Bolder: one continuous zoom from dot to thumbnail to full page, each odd page's thread as long as its measured pixel difference.

## Ranking and hybrid
Rank by client love: C, B, D, A.
Best hybrid: C's pigeonhole frame and stamp rituals holding B's paper reams with their riffle, printed in the riso inks (riso blue ground, odd and failed pages stamped in pink as the off-register plate, D's "% alike" as the stamp text). It keeps C's new skeleton and rituals, B's clear reams and best motion, and the product's own identity.

Gap to 9/9: A needs a new skeleton and real captures. B needs a skeleton that isn't a grid, and one accent. C needs about 40% less noise and one lighting pass. D needs to show pages, in a look that isn't the embedding-galaxy cliché.
