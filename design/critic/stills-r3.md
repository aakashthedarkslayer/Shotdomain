What breaks the illusion:
1. Half the bed is placeholder: Product a blank "Next in line" box, One-off an empty dashed square, Home and Pricing 26 px specks.
2. The biggest object is mush: Blog's face is a pale dot field; Docs is denser, so the bigger pile looks paler.
3. The insight is crammed: five 127 px odd sheets tilted and overlapping; "the odder the higher" spans 46 px, so the ranking is invisible; the pink reads as highlights, not a slip.
4. The screen needs a "How to read it." legend.
5. Two totals 700 px apart ("412 pages in 6 templates", "312 of 404").
6. The diff encodes the wrong thing: /blog/benchmarks (broken, 83% alike) slips 4 px; /blog/old-theme-post (a redesign) slips 18 px. Different ranks above broken.
7. The opened view leaves the print world: true-colour rows with pink rectangles; the missing-image box frames the headline; the benchmarks iPhone frame looks failed; pink means picked (chips) and broken (rows) in one view; "All piles" reads as an eyebrow.
8. Start: six empty boxes (first-glance cap 4) and dashed final outlines the crawler cannot know.

Motion 6: landing sheets fade with no settle and transfer no ink; the drum reads as a slider thumb and moves linearly; the plate slip overshoots (a plate slips and sticks); opening a pile is an instant swap; the odd-sheet hover is unproven in the frames.

Top fixes:
1. Make the overprint the actual diff: pink plate = the pile's median ink map (AM 75 degrees), blue plate = the page (AM 45 degrees), multiplied in register with a fixed 2 px fringe. Both inks make violet; pink alone is what the template has and the page lacks; blue alone is what the page added. Drop the likeness offset. In the opened view, the same pink plate at 35% multiply over the true-colour frames behind a "Template plate" chip, instead of rectangles.
2. Clean, dense pile faces: below 0.12 coverage no dot, 0.12 to 0.8 AM 45 degrees at 6 px, above 0.8 solid; normalise tone per pile; dot jitter, 180 px mottle, 4% grain; 2 to 4 hairline sheet edges by log n.
3. Fill the empty half: Product queued prints its 48 URLs as a manifest; One-off as 8 small single-ink prints; Home and Pricing 56 px minimum; start plates fill with one 3 px dot per URL, no final outline.
4. Odd sheets as a ranked row: 176 px, 16 px gaps, at most 1 degree; height above the pile by unlikeness x 160 px with a thread to where the diff sits; page-check failures print past the sheet's trim, so broken and different are two print faults.
5. Three sizes (76, 19, 15), one number system, no legend: sky "312 printed. 5 don't line up."; bar "412 found, 404 picked"; "96 of 140" on the drum; one leader-lined note on the first odd sheet; "All piles" into the bar as a path.

Bolder version: the whole site is one press sheet (an A2 riso imposition with crop marks and colour bars), the templates print first as the pink key plate, every page prints over its plate in blue; pages that line up turn the pink violet, pages that don't leave raw pink "unpaired ink" exactly where they differ, so a finished capture is a violet sheet with a few pink scars, and the scars are the bugs. A square loupe over a scar shows the true-colour page; dragging a plate back into register accepts the difference (forks a template); the sheet folds into the share link.

Gap to 9/9: two points on each axis. Keep the mechanic, the real data and the two-ink language. Nine needs one still where the overprint itself shows, without words, which pages break and where (a template plate under the page, pink only where they differ), the frame filled edge to edge with crisp textured ink, and the opened view continuing the same sheet.
